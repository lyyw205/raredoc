The '''S-P Promotional cards''' are a series of Japanese promotional cards tied in with the Sword & Shield Era of the [[Pokémon Trading Card Game]], which began on November 29, 2019 with the release of the {{TCG|V Starter Sets}}. All promotional cards released during this Era have the suffix '''S-P'''.
